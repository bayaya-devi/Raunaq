# RAUNAQ 30-Day Content Calendar

## Content Pillars
- Product clarity: pot, label, weight, texture, packaging.
- Buying clarity: price, order steps, delivery confirmation.
- Responsible education: how to read a supplement label, storage and professional advice.
- Brand craft: design details, preparation and behind-the-scenes.
- Conversation: genuine questions, polls and customer feedback.

## Publishing Rhythm

## Creative Rules
- Use the real product in the first two seconds.
- Show one message per asset.
- Add readable Arabic/French captions; do not rely on audio.
- Never show fabricated testimonials or medical before/after imagery.
- Every performance asset ships in two variants: factual hook vs question hook.
- Use UTMs: `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`, `utm_content`.
