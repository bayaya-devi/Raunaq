# RAUNAQ Brand Voice

## Core Voice
RAUNAQ is refined, direct, reassuring and culturally fluent. It speaks with confidence but does not make medical promises.

## Message Hierarchy
1. Product: RAUNAQ Formule Royale, pot 250 g.
2. Experience: clear price, direct ordering request, multilingual assistance.
3. Trust: real product images, transparent information, reachable social channels.
4. Safety: food supplement, not medicine; professional advice remains appropriate.

## Writing Rules
- Lead with the concrete fact or customer action.
- Use short paragraphs and one CTA.
- Keep claims limited to what the packaging, product file or verified evidence supports.
- Never invent reviews, scarcity, certifications, clinical results or customer counts.
- Avoid marketing jargon, absolute promises and fear-based copy.
- Preserve Arabic-only visible interface text in Arabic mode.

## Approved Vocabulary
Premium, selected, clear, direct, carefully presented, royal formula, net weight, order request.

## Avoid
Cure, treatment, guaranteed result, clinically proven without documentation, number one, miracle, instant effect.
