# RAUNAQ Design System

## Positioning
RAUNAQ is a premium Moroccan wellness storefront. The interface should feel precise, calm and trustworthy, never clinical or exaggerated.

## Visual Tokens
- Primary: `#14322b` deep green for brand and primary actions.
- Accent: `#c49b55` restrained gold for emphasis.
- Background: `#f7f2e8` ivory and `#fffdf8` paper.
- Support: `#e9eee8` mist and `#66806c` sage.
- Headings: Cairo. Body: Tajawal in Arabic, Inter in Latin languages.
- Radius: 6-12 px. Avoid decorative card nesting and oversized rounded surfaces.

## Interaction
- Primary CTA: one clear action per section.
- Hover: subtle lift and contrast increase.
- Keyboard: visible focus ring on every interactive control.
- Motion: parallax and float remain optional and respect reduced-motion preferences.
- Responsive: mobile page is selected automatically at 760 px and below.

## Image Direction
Show the real pot, label and ingredient material. Use transparent cutouts, neutral light and full-product framing. Do not use unrelated lifestyle stock photography as primary evidence.
