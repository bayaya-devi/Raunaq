## Influencer Brief
Target micro-creators whose audience is primarily Moroccan and whose content covers men's wellness, premium local products or responsible lifestyle.

Required deliverable:
- One 20-35 second vertical video.
- Product visible in first two seconds.
- Creator states personal experience only; no universal health claim.
- Price and sponsored relationship disclosed clearly.
- Raw footage and 30-day usage rights defined contractually.

Fit score: audience alignment 40%, content quality 25%, authentic engagement 20%, compliance history 15%. Reject creators with suspicious engagement or recurring medical claims.

## Email Lifecycle Drafts
Use only after explicit opt-in and a verified sender domain.

### Email 1 - Request Received
Subject A: We received your RAUNAQ request
Subject B: Your RAUNAQ reference
Content: Confirm product, quantity and reference. Explain that availability, delivery and total will be confirmed separately.

### Email 2 - Product Information
Send after 2 days if no purchase confirmation.
Content: Real product images, 250 g format, safety notice, direct contact link.

### Email 3 - Post-Delivery Check
Send 3 days after confirmed delivery.
Content: Ask whether the order arrived correctly. Invite private support; request a review only after resolving issues.

### Email 4 - Reorder Reminder
Timing must be based on genuine consumption guidance approved for the product. Include one-click unsubscribe.
